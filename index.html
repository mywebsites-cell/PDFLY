Help & Tips

  • Word → PDF / PDF → Word: For best results, upload DOCX files and keep them under 50 MB.
  • Image → PDF: Use JPG or PNG; choose high resolution if you need print-quality output.
  • Compress: Works best for image-heavy documents – try different quality settings.
  • Privacy: Client-side tools run in your browser. Server conversions (Word↔PDF) are processed on your server and auto-deleted.
