Common Use Cases for Page Numbering

Adding page numbers improves document organization, facilitates navigation, and maintains professional standards for formal documents.

  • Book & E-Book Publishing: Add sequential page numbers to book manuscripts, e-books, and published materials
  • Academic Papers: Number thesis, dissertation, and research paper pages for proper academic formatting
  • Legal Documents: Add page numbers to contracts, agreements, and legal briefs for precise cross-referencing
  • Manual Documentation: Number technical manuals, user guides, and operating instructions for easy reference
  • Multi-Document Projects: Add custom page numbering to merged documents to maintain continuous numbering across sections
