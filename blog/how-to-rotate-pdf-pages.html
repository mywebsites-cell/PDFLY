How to Rotate PDF Pages — Free Guide (2026)

Published: January 29, 2026 • 5 min read

Incorrectly oriented PDF pages are a common frustration — especially from scanned documents or PDFs exported from mobile devices. Fixing page rotation is quick and free. This guide covers all the best methods for rotating individual pages or entire PDFs.

Why Do PDF Pages End Up Sideways?

Pages often need rotation due to:

  • Scanner orientation: Documents placed sideways or upside-down on a flatbed scanner
  • Phone camera scans: Documents photographed in landscape when the source was portrait
  • Exported landscapes: Wide spreadsheets or presentations exported as landscape pages inside a portrait PDF
  • Mixed-orientation documents: Documents with both portrait and landscape pages that need consistent orientation
  • Legacy files: Old PDFs with incorrect metadata rotation flags that display incorrectly in modern viewers

Method 2: Adobe Acrobat

Adobe Acrobat Reader (the free version) lets you view and temporarily rotate pages, but only Acrobat Pro permanently saves the rotation.

Rotate Pages Permanently in Acrobat Pro:

  1. Open your PDF in Adobe Acrobat Pro
  2. Go to Tools ? Organize Pages
  3. In the page thumbnail panel, select the page(s) you want to rotate (Ctrl+click for multiple)
  4. Click the Rotate Clockwise (?) or Rotate Counter-clockwise (?) button in the toolbar
  5. Save the file (Ctrl+S or File ? Save)

Important: In Acrobat Reader (free version), you can rotate pages temporarily for viewing using View ? Rotate View, but this rotation is NOT saved when you close the file. You need Acrobat Pro to permanently save rotations.

Method 3: Preview on Mac (Built-In, Free)

Mac's Preview app can permanently rotate individual PDF pages for free.

How to Rotate Pages in Preview:

  1. Open your PDF in Preview
  2. Show the thumbnail sidebar: View ? Thumbnails (Cmd+Shift+1)
  3. Click the page thumbnail you want to rotate
  4. Press Cmd+L to rotate left (counter-clockwise) or Cmd+R to rotate right (clockwise)
  5. For multiple pages: Cmd+click to select multiple thumbnails, then rotate all at once
  6. Save: File ? Save (Cmd+S)

Pros: Built into macOS, completely free, intuitive keyboard shortcuts
Cons: Mac only

Method 4: Google Chrome (View-Only Rotation)

You can rotate a PDF in Google Chrome's built-in viewer, but like Acrobat Reader, this rotation is temporary and not saved.

  1. Open your PDF in Chrome (drag and drop onto a Chrome tab)
  2. Click the rotate button (?) in the toolbar at the top right
  3. Each click rotates the view by 90°

To permanently save the rotation, use PDFLY or one of the desktop methods above.

Rotation Options Explained

Rotation What It Does When to Use
90° Clockwise Turns page right (landscape becomes portrait) Page is sideways to the left
90° Counter-clockwise Turns page left Page is sideways to the right
180° Flips page upside down Page is completely upside down

Common Questions About PDF Rotation

Does rotating a PDF reduce its quality?

No. PDF rotation changes only the rotation metadata of the page — it doesn't re-render or re-compress any content. Text sharpness, image resolution, and font quality all remain identical to the original.

Can I rotate just one page while keeping the others unchanged?

Yes. PDFLY lets you select which pages to rotate. You can rotate a single page, specific pages, only odd/even pages, or all pages independently.

My PDF has some portrait and some landscape pages. How do I fix it?

This is a mixed-orientation document. The best approach is to rotate only the incorrectly oriented pages rather than all pages. Use PDFLY's per-page rotation selection to fix individual pages without affecting the correctly-oriented ones.

Why does my rotated PDF still appear sideways in some apps?

Some older PDF viewers ignore the rotation metadata and display pages at their raw orientation. Ensure you're using a modern PDF viewer (Chrome, Adobe Reader, Preview on Mac) that respects rotation metadata. If the problem persists, the rotation wasn't saved permanently — use PDFLY or Acrobat Pro to save it properly.
