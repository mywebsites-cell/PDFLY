How to Add a Watermark to a PDF — Free Guide (2026)

Published: January 27, 2026 • 6 min read

Watermarks are a powerful way to protect your intellectual property, mark document status, or brand your PDFs. Whether you need to stamp "CONFIDENTIAL" on internal reports or add your company logo to client deliverables, this guide shows you how to add watermarks for free.

Why Add Watermarks to PDFs?

  • Copyright protection: Discourage unauthorized copying or redistribution of your content
  • Document status: Mark documents as DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, FOR REVIEW, or SAMPLE
  • Brand identity: Add your logo or company name to every page of client-facing PDFs
  • Version control: Mark specific document versions to avoid confusion between revisions
  • Proof distribution: Add watermarks to proofs before final approval to prevent premature use

Method 2: Adobe Acrobat Pro

Adobe Acrobat Pro offers the most advanced watermarking options, including image watermarks, per-page control, and print-only watermarks.

How to Add a Watermark in Adobe Acrobat:

  1. Open your PDF in Adobe Acrobat Pro
  2. Go to Tools ? Edit PDF ? Watermark ? Add
  3. Choose "Text" or "File" (for image watermarks)
  4. For text: Enter your watermark text, set font, size, color, and opacity
  5. For image: Browse for your logo file (JPG, PNG, or PDF)
  6. Set position (center, corner) and rotation
  7. Use "Page Range Options" to apply to specific pages only
  8. Click "OK" and save

Advanced options: Acrobat can make watermarks appear only when printing (not on screen), which is useful for print proofs.

Cons: Requires Adobe Acrobat Pro subscription ($19.99/month)

Method 3: LibreOffice Writer/Impress (Free Desktop)

LibreOffice can add watermarks to documents before exporting them as PDFs. This works best if you still have the source document (not a scanned PDF).

Adding a Watermark in LibreOffice Writer:

  1. Open your document in LibreOffice Writer
  2. Go to Format ? Watermark
  3. Enter your watermark text and configure font, color, and transparency
  4. Click "OK" — the watermark appears on all pages
  5. Export as PDF: File ? Export as PDF

Understanding Watermark Settings

Opacity

Opacity controls how visible the watermark is relative to the document content. Common settings:

  • 10–20%: Subtle — barely visible, professional-looking on printed output
  • 30–50%: Moderate — clearly visible but document content remains readable
  • 70–100%: Heavy — very obvious, used for "SAMPLE" or "VOID" stamps

Rotation Angle

Most watermarks use a 45° diagonal rotation. This is the hardest position to remove with simple cropping and covers the most page area. For header or footer watermarks, use 0° (horizontal).

Position

  • Center diagonal: Best for copyright protection — covers the most visual area
  • Top header: Good for document status labels (DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL)
  • Bottom footer: Good for branding (company name, website URL)
  • Corner stamp: Good for version numbers or approval stamps

Common Questions About PDF Watermarks

Can watermarks be removed from a PDF?

Text and image watermarks embedded via PDF tools like PDFLY or Acrobat can be challenging to remove cleanly. However, they are not 100% permanent — determined users with advanced PDF editors can sometimes remove them. For truly tamper-proof protection, combine watermarks with PDF password protection.

Do watermarks affect the original PDF quality?

No. Adding a watermark overlays a new layer on the PDF — the original content underneath is not modified or re-compressed. The original document quality is fully preserved.

Can I add a watermark to just one page?

Yes. Adobe Acrobat Pro and some online tools let you apply watermarks to specific page ranges (e.g., pages 1–3 only). PDFLY currently applies watermarks to all pages.

Can I add a logo as a watermark instead of text?

Yes — Adobe Acrobat Pro supports image watermarks (JPG, PNG). For logo watermarks, use a transparent PNG file for the cleanest result.
