Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rotate PDF free?

Yes. 100% free, no sign-up, no watermarks.

What rotation angles are supported?

You can rotate pages 90, 180 or 270 degrees clockwise.

Can I rotate specific pages only?

Currently all pages are rotated by the same angle. Per-page rotation is coming soon.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Rotation is done entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Will rotation affect the text or images in my PDF?

No. Only the page orientation changes. All content remains intact and at full quality.
