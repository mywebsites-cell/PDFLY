Help & Tips

  • Testing: Test with a phone camera before publishing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the QR Code Generator free?

Yes. 100% free, no sign-up, no limits on how many QR codes you generate.

What can I encode in a QR code?

URLs, plain text, email addresses, phone numbers and contact information (vCard).

Are my QR codes saved on your server?

No. QR codes are generated entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

What format is the QR code downloaded in?

QR codes are downloaded as a PDF file ready for print or digital use.

What size are the generated QR codes?

QR codes are generated at print-ready resolution suitable for business cards, posters and digital documents.
