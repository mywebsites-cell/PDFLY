Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image to PDF free?

Yes. 100% free, no sign-up, no watermarks.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP and WebP are all supported.

Can I add multiple images?

Yes. Upload multiple images and they will be placed on separate pages in the PDF in the order you select them.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image to PDF conversion happens entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device.

Can I choose the page size?

The PDF page is sized to fit each image. For print output, use high-resolution source images (300 DPI or above).
