Frequently Asked Questions

Is the PDF merge tool completely free?

Yes. PDFLY's Merge PDF tool is 100% free with no sign-up, no watermarks and no file limits.

Are my PDF files private when I merge them?

Yes. Merging is done entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

How many PDF files can I merge at once?

You can merge as many PDF files as your browser can handle. There is no strict limit imposed by PDFLY.

Can I reorder pages before merging?

Yes. Use the up and down arrows next to each file to set the merge order before combining them.

Will merging PDFs reduce their quality?

No. PDFLY merges PDFs without re-encoding or compressing them so the output quality is identical to the originals.
