Frequently Asked Questions

Is Reorder Pages free?

Yes. 100% free, no sign-up, no watermarks.

How do I reorder pages?

Upload your PDF and drag the page thumbnails into your desired order, then click Save.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Reordering is done entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Can I delete pages while reordering?

Yes. You can remove individual pages before saving the reordered PDF.

Is there a page limit?

No. You can reorder PDFs with any number of pages directly in your browser.
