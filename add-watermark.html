Frequently Asked Questions

Is Add Watermark free?

Yes. 100% free, no sign-up required.

Can I customize the watermark text?

Yes. You can set your own text, choose position (center, top, bottom), adjust opacity and select font size.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Watermarking is done entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Can I add an image watermark instead of text?

Currently only text watermarks are supported. Image watermarks are planned for a future update.

Will the watermark affect the rest of the PDF content?

No. The watermark is added as a transparent overlay. All existing content remains fully intact.
